01Predictive analytics

Predictive Analysis of Gym Member Calorie Expenditure

A modelling project exploring how member characteristics and activity relate to calorie expenditure. The workflow covered data preparation, model comparison and recommendations.

My contribution

Cleaned data, handled duplicates, encoded categorical variables and developed Linear Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest models. Compared model evaluation results and identified four significant predictors in the final Linear Regression model.

The longer project record reports 4,865 source rows reduced to 973 unique records and an R-squared of 0.98 for the final model. This is the reported evaluation result and does not by itself establish performance on unseen data.

KNIMESAS ViyaSAS Visual AnalyticsSAS Visual Statistics
